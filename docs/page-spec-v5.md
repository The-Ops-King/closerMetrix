# CloserMetrix.com — Page Spec v5

Final. Ten blocks. Supersedes v4.

**The two rules that make this page work.**

Every block gets a maximum of two sentences of body copy. If a block needs a third, it needed a picture instead.

Half these blocks show the product rather than describe it. A page reads alive when the eye has somewhere to land between headlines. Icons don't do that. Product does.

**The arc.** Recognition, then curiosity, then fear, then relief, then proof, then price. He should understand what you do inside ten seconds and want the next block every time he finishes one.

**H2**
You bought a recorder. You needed a reader.

*Two columns. Left muted grey, right full contrast. No icons, no checkmarks, no cards. Rows align.*

| Today | With CloserMetrix |
|---|---|
| Recordings pile up in a folder | Every call read, same day |
| Notes typed from memory between calls | Notes taken from what was actually said |

| You hear about the bad promise at the refund | You hear about it Tuesday afternoon |
| You guess what buyers want | You know, in their words, every month |

**One line under the table**
Nothing here needs a person.

**Why this block carries no body copy:** he runs the comparison himself in four seconds. Explaining it afterward insults him and adds the exact weight you're trying to remove.

**Why "one call in ten":** a diligent owner reads "four calls a week" and opts out because his manager does more. One in ten applies even to the manager who's genuinely on top of it.

**Never add a third column for a competitor.** The moment one appears this becomes a comparison page, and comparison pages argue you're better. The position is that you're different.

## Design direction

**The problem with the current build.** Every block is a gradient headline over two paragraphs of grey body, centered, ten times. Nothing varies, so the eye never lands and the page reads as an essay with headers.

**What varies now.** Table, split image, Slack mockup, big-number row, timeline, checklist. Six different shapes in ten blocks. No two adjacent blocks share a layout.

**Gradient headlines.** Use on three at most. Right now every headline is gradient, which means none of them are emphasized. Suggest hero, depth, and the price reveal.

**Body copy.** Grey on near-black at that size is hard work. Lift the contrast and cap line length around 65 characters.

**Icons.** None. The old site had them everywhere and it read as decoration. Product screenshots and real structure do what icons pretend to do.

## Build notes

**Navigation.** Currently lists Features, How It Works, and FAQ, none of which exist. Change to What You Get, Pricing, Book a Demo.

**Retire "The Sales Intelligence Layer."** Meta title: CloserMetrix — Sales Integrity Audits for High-Ticket Teams

**Footer.** Privacy Policy and Terms need real documents. You process recordings containing other people's financial details. Delete About, Blog, and Careers unless they go somewhere.

**Add a data handling page.** Storage, access, retention, deletion on request, whether you train on client data. Every serious buyer asks all five.
